How long does fence installation take?

Most projects are completed within two to five days, depending on the fence type, property size, weather, and whether it's a residential or commercial project. We'll give you a timeline before we begin.

Can you remove my old fence?

Yes. We remove and dispose of your existing fence as part of the project, and it's included in your quote.

What is the most durable fence for Ohio weather?

Vinyl, composite and aluminum all hold up very well to Central Ohio's freeze-thaw cycles. We'll help you weigh appearance, maintenance and cost during your estimate.

What's the best fence for dogs?

It depends on your dog's size and behavior. We typically recommend privacy fences for dogs that react to passersby, and chain link or containment fencing for an economical, secure option.

Is Elite Fence licensed and insured?

Yes. Elite Fence is licensed, bonded and insured, holds Columbus City General Contractor licensing, and has Nursery Dealer Certification from the Ohio Department of Agriculture.
